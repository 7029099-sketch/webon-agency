Google Ads for international service businesses

WebON Agency works remotely with English-speaking businesses that want enquiries from search, not just a larger click count. Campaign planning starts with the service being sold, the country where customers can be served, and the action that counts as a lead. Search advertising for the United Kingdom, United States, Canada or Australia should reflect local demand and service availability rather than reuse one generic keyword list for every market.

Markets and search intent

We separate countries and service areas when language, competition or budgets call for it. Location settings, ad schedules, search terms and landing-page promises are checked against where the business can genuinely serve customers.

Lead quality over raw traffic

We distinguish requests for a quotation or consultation from informational searches, tutorials, employment queries and people looking for software. Negative keywords are reviewed carefully so valuable commercial terms remain eligible.

Measurement and attribution

We align GA4 events, contact forms, calls and campaign tracking with the enquiry process. Conversion counts require working measurement, so zero tracked conversions are investigated before drawing conclusions about sales.

What happens when we set up a search campaign?

First we review your offer, ideal customer, service locations and website. Next we propose campaign groups, relevant keywords, ad messages and the corresponding landing pages. We agree on the advertising budget separately from the setup and management work. After launch we examine real search queries and the cost and quality of enquiries, then make evidence-led refinements to keywords, negatives, ad copy and landing pages.

This approach is suitable for international B2B services, professional service firms and companies serving customers remotely. For products or complex multi-country campaigns, we assess the account structure and conversion setup individually before recommending an approach. Google decides ad delivery and ranking; no specific leads or return can be guaranteed.

Useful next steps: review our GA4 and conversion tracking service, improve an existing business landing page, or discuss your target market with the team.
