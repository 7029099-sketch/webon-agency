GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Do you work with businesses in the US and UK?

We are building our international service offering for companies in the United States and United Kingdom. We work remotely and plan the project around your goals and time zone.

How much does a website cost?

Pricing depends on page count, functionality, content, integrations and timeline. We prepare a tailored estimate after reviewing your requirements.

Can you help with SEO and advertising?

Yes. We can plan search-friendly site architecture, technical SEO, Google Ads and conversion tracking as separate scopes or a combined project.

How long does a website take to build?

Simple landing pages may move quickly with approved content. More complex business websites and stores need discovery, design, development and testing time.

Can you maintain the site after launch?

Yes. Ongoing support, improvements and digital marketing assistance can be arranged separately.
