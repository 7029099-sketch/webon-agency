VideoLive: Distinct service pages and traceable enquiries

VideoLive combines professional filming and event streaming, but the buyers and search terms for those services are not identical. We organized the site into separate service paths and added technical controls around enquiries and publishing.

2 key pathsFilming and live streaming
ExperienceConference and project evidence
Lead contextPage, referrer and campaign parameters
SEO operationsSitemaps and automated site checks

Separating commercial topics

Filming, multi-camera event coverage, hybrid conferences and live streaming are described on distinct pages rather than repeated in a single general sales pitch.

Supporting each service with evidence

A project and experience section gives visitors technical context on production workflows, including conference filming and a documented long-form streaming project. Only details reported by the team are published.

Handling enquiries

The site's lead flow records available attribution context, including entry page, referrer and UTM parameters. Successful delivery to the configured endpoint is distinguished from client-side submission attempts.

Automated technical checks

The repository includes deployment and daily checks for important URLs and SEO files. A sitemap for video content and a separate standard sitemap support discovery.

A deliberately separate website

VideoLive has its own site structure and analytics. It is not treated as a mirror of another production site and should not rely on artificial links between related brands.

What this project demonstrates

Search visibility starts with understandable services, relevant evidence and accurate technical signals.

Focused landing pages

Direct visitors to the specific filming or broadcast service they need.

Accountable enquiries

Preserve campaign and landing-page context without inventing conversion results.

Maintenance with evidence

Keep technical checks and release logs attached to actual site changes.

This case covers implemented website architecture and technical processes. It does not claim organic ranking growth or conversion uplift without validated measurement.
