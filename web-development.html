Website development FAQ

Answers about website development, pricing, timelines, SEO and advertising setup.

How much does a website cost?

Landing pages start at $300, business websites start at $800, and e-commerce stores start at $1,300. Final pricing depends on scope, design and integrations.

How long does website development take?

A simple landing page can be launched in 1–3 days. Corporate websites usually take 3–10 days and e-commerce stores from 7–13 days.

What is included in full-cycle website development?

Structure, responsive design, front-end development, forms, basic SEO structure, SSL, analytics connection and advertising-ready setup.

Can I order a business website with a CMS?

Yes. Corporate websites and e-commerce stores can include a CMS so you can edit text, images, products and selected sections yourself.

Is SEO included in website development?

Basic technical SEO structure is included. Ongoing SEO, keyword expansion and content planning can be added separately.

Can the website be prepared for Google Ads and Meta Ads?

Yes. We configure forms and events, connect analytics and prepare landing pages for paid traffic.
