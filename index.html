Frequently asked questions

Short answers about websites, SEO, advertising and analytics.

How much does a full website cost?

Landing pages start at $300, business websites start at $800, and e-commerce stores start at $1,300. Final pricing depends on page count, content, integrations and advertising infrastructure.

Can a website be launched in 3 business days?

Yes, for a compact project with an approved structure and ready content. More complex corporate websites and stores require more time.

Is the website ready for Google Ads and Meta Ads?

Yes. We prepare responsive structure, forms, GA4, conversion events, UTM tracking and Meta Pixel when needed so paid traffic can be measured.

What is included in basic SEO preparation?

Logical page structure, Title and Description, headings, canonical tags, sitemap.xml, robots.txt, Schema.org, internal linking and clear indexable content.

Can I get support after launch?

Yes. WebON can continue technical maintenance, analytics, lead checks and advertising campaign management after the website is published.
