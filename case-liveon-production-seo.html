LiveOn Production: Website structure, indexable services and repeatable releases

A production company needs more than a general portfolio. Potential clients search for corporate filming, live broadcasts and business video, each with a different goal. Our job was to make those services understandable to visitors and search crawlers while keeping site updates traceable.

Service mapSeparate filming, streaming and content pages
Search hygieneCanonical, metadata and structured data
DeliveryVersioned releases through GitHub
ChecksMonitoring for key site URLs

The challenge

Business-video requests and live-stream enquiries should not depend on one broad homepage. We audited the site structure against the distinct services the production team actually offers.

What changed in the site

Dedicated service and case URLs make it easier to link a prospective client directly to an appropriate offer. Homepage and supporting content point visitors toward the relevant service page.

Technical SEO foundation

Page-specific titles, descriptions, canonical URLs, structured data, robots rules and a sitemap form the crawling baseline. Those signals help search engines understand the content; they do not guarantee rankings.

Publication safeguards

GitHub keeps a change history, and deployment checks look for key pages and essential SEO markup. We corrected a separate analytics-ID mismatch in the automated daily health test without changing the live site's analytics.

Verifiable evidence

The project can be inspected in its published form. Claimed rankings or lead growth are deliberately excluded until reliable Search Console and analytics reports are available.

What this project demonstrates

Search visibility starts with understandable services, relevant evidence and accurate technical signals.

Specific search intent

Filming, social content and live transmission belong on their relevant service pages.

Real production work

Portfolio items and production cases give concrete context to service descriptions.

Repeatable operations

SEO checks become part of the release process, not a one-off launch task.

This case covers implemented website architecture and technical processes. It does not claim organic ranking growth or conversion uplift without validated measurement.
