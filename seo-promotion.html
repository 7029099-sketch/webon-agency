SEO questions

What is included in SEO promotion?

Technical audit, keyword mapping, metadata, H1-H3 structure, landing pages, internal linking, content, sitemap, robots and indexation monitoring.

How soon can SEO produce results?

SEO compounds over time. Timing depends on competition, the current condition of the site, domain history, useful page coverage and indexation speed.

SEO or Google Ads?

Ads can generate traffic faster. SEO gradually builds durable organic visibility. Many businesses benefit from using both channels together.
